## What changed since Week 1

- **Week 2:** Noelle beat Henry (198.9–156.6), Lauren beat Emma (186.9–158.2), Raghav beat Conrad (166.9–141.3) and Ben beat Avery (214.8–179.0). The 2-0/0-2 records are now worth about ±2.5–3 points of title odds.
- **Lauren lost Jaxson Dart for the season** (knee surgery), and Josh Jacobs is still on the exempt list. Hurts is her only QB, so Week 10 is a zero at QB.
- **Henry's pass catchers are hurt:** Reed (out to Week 12), A.J. Brown (to Week 7), Njoku (to 6), Goedert (to 5), Collins (Week 3).
- **Noelle's core is running hot:** JSN had 42.5 in Week 2 and Mahomes 26.7/35.0. Daniels (elbow, to Week 5) and Pierce (to Week 8) are out, but her roster absorbs it.
- **Ben rose the most on roster strength:** Prescott had 37.8 and Lamb 35.3 in Week 2. His one risk is Stevenson losing snaps to Henderson.
- **Emma swapped Caleb Williams (hamstring) for Tyler Shough** for 121 FAAB.

## Team by team

- **Noelle (25.8%):** 2-0 on 395.0 points and ahead on paper in 11 of her 12 remaining games. Daniels (out Weeks 3–5) and Pierce are hurt, but they were her depth, not her core.
- **Raghav (20.6%):** 2-0, but the lowest scorer of the three unbeaten teams. Josh Allen (39.7, 46.8) carries a WR room that ranks 7th of 8. Week 7 vs Noelle (Allen on bye) is the danger game.
- **Ben (13.9%):** the quiet riser. The league's best best-ball converter, with a league-high 214.8 in Week 2. His Week 10 and 14 byes land in the seeding stretch.
- **Emma (13.7%):** still the deepest backfield, with the WR room still last. She gave back Week 1's windfall and swapped an injured Caleb Williams for Tyler Shough.
- **Henry (9.9%):** 0-2 and 8 of the league's 40 injury flags, but still one of the biggest point piles on paper. The injuries all fall early, and he projects as a top-three weekly scorer in Weeks 11–14.
- **Lauren (7.9%):** 2-0 with the most points (400.3), but Dart's injury leaves her one QB, so Week 10 is empty at QB and Week 13 empty at RB. The record is carrying a 7th-place roster.
- **Conrad (6.6%):** the unluckiest 0-2 (301.9 PF, last by 32.6). Every engine rates his roster above Lauren's; the record, not the roster, drops him to 7th.
- **Avery (1.6%):** last in all 12 runs. His QBs are now a strength, but his RB/TE starters are the league's worst, and he's the underdog on paper in all 12 remaining games.
